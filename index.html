🛡️ Disaster Safety Precautions & Protocols

Earthquake Survival Protocol

  • Drop, Cover, and Hold On: Immediately drop beneath a sturdy table or desk.
  • Stay Indoors: Do not run outdoors while the ground is shaking; protect against falling masonry and glass.
  • Shield Your Head: If shelter is unavailable, curl into an inside corner of the building and cover your head with your arms.

Tsunami Evacuation Protocol

  • Evacuate Coastlines Immediately: Head inland or to ground at least 30 meters above sea level right after coastal shaking.
  • Watch For Rapid Shoreline Drawback: If ocean water recedes rapidly, do not walk out to investigate. Evacuate immediately.
  • Multi-Wave Alert: Tsunami wave trains can arrive over several hours; never return after the first wave.

Cyclone & Hurricane Protocol

  • Secure Outer Structures: Fasten exterior items, storm shutters, and heavy doors.
  • Stay Away from Glass: Move to an interior room without external windows.
  • Cut Main Utilities: Switch off main circuit breakers and gas mains if water starts rising.

Volcano Eruption & Ashfall Protocol

  • Shield Respiratory Passages: Wear an N95 respirator mask or damp cloth to filter abrasive silicate ash particles.
  • Seal Living Spaces: Close windows, doors, and air dampers; cover gaps with tape.
  • Avoid Low-Lying Riverbeds: Stay clear of river valleys downstream of volcanic calderas to prevent entrapment by lahars.

Flood & Inundation Protocol

  • Never Walk or Drive Through Moving Waters: 30 cm of fast water is sufficient to float most vehicles.
  • Seek High Elevation: Move to upper building levels rather than attics without roof exits.
  • Avoid Contact With Flood Water: Submerged electrical circuits and ruptured sewage mains create severe health hazards.
